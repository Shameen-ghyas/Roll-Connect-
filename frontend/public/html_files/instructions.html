INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Select your theme: SPACE or FOREST before starting.
  2. Choose number of players: 2 or 4 in offline mode, or create/join a room for online mode.
  3. Move your pawns using arrow keys or W, A, S, D keys.
  4. Click the dice to roll and move the corresponding number of spaces.
  5. Objective: Move all your pawns from start to home before your opponents do.
